Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how the Investments Android app (“the app”) handles information. In short: your records stay on your phone, and we do not collect them.

1. Information the app stores

You type the information yourself, for example names of people, businesses and investments, amounts, dates, profit shares and payments. The app keeps it:

  • in a database on your own phone;
  • in backup files that you create and decide where to keep;
  • if you turn it on, in your own Google Drive (see section 3).

The app does not need you to create an account with us. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not run any server for this app. The developer does not receive, see, store or sell your records, your contacts, your location or any other personal information.

3. Google Drive sync and backups (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app signs you in with your Google account and uses these permissions:

PermissionWhat it is used for
drive.appdata A hidden folder that belongs only to this app in your Google Drive. The app keeps its sync files there so your own phones can share the same data. Other apps cannot read it and you do not see it in your normal Drive.
drive.file Lets the app create and manage only the files it created itself, such as the daily backup files in a folder called “Investments Backups”. The app cannot open your other files.

The app does not read the rest of your Drive. Your email address is shown inside the app to tell you which account is connected. You can disconnect at any time from More → Backup & Data, and you can remove the app’s access in your Google Account permissions.

The app’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Security

  • The app is protected by a PIN, and by your fingerprint if your phone supports it. The PIN is stored only as a salted hash in your phone’s secure storage, never in plain text.
  • Data sent to Google Drive travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Backup files contain your records in readable form. Keep them somewhere safe.

5. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone. The app only shares something when you use a Share, Print, Image, PDF, Excel or Backup button, and then it goes to the app or place that you pick.

6. Permissions on your phone

  • Internet – only for the optional Google Drive sync.
  • Fingerprint / biometrics – only to unlock the app.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. More → Backup & Data → Clear all data erases everything in the app. Uninstalling the app removes the data on that phone. To delete the sync files from Google Drive, disconnect Google Drive in the app and remove the app in your Google Account permissions.

8. Children

The app is a financial record-keeping tool and is not meant for children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
