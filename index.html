Investments

A simple, private app to keep your investments, profit sharing and payments in order – on your own phone.

What the app does

Investments is for people who invest money in businesses, products or orders, sometimes together with other members, and need a clear record of who put in how much, what profit came back, how it was split and who has been paid.

InvestmentsRecord what you invest in, who put in the money, and your share of the profit.
Profit sharingSplit each profit by capital or by your own percentages. Totals always add up exactly.
PaymentsSee who is waiting for money, record every payment, and clear a mistake.
StatementsA clear statement and ledger for every member. Share as an image or PDF, or print.
Works offlineAll data is stored on your phone. No account is needed to use the app.
Backup & syncOptional Google Drive sync between your own phones, plus backup and restore files.

Your data stays yours

  • Your records are stored on your phone and are protected by a PIN and fingerprint lock.
  • We do not run any server and we do not receive your data.
  • If you turn on Google Drive sync, your data goes only to your own Google Drive, in a private area of the app.
